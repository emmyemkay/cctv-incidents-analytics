6
Conclusion and operational use

How to Use the Map Analytics

The interactive report improves on static maps by allowing administrators to change the period, source, Category, station and coordinate scope without producing a new document.

Deployment planningUse category concentration and station hotspots to prioritize patrols and specialist resources.
Targeted analysisOpen individual Category layers to distinguish the geography of breakings, traffic, theft, homicide and other incidents.
Data improvementUse the unmapped count and coverage table to identify sources or Categories requiring better coordinate capture.
Trend follow-upApply date filters and compare map layers with hotspot, outcome and forecast workspaces before acting.
